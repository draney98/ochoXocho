Touch/Drag Settings

Control zone height
How much of the screen (from the bottom) uses reach amplification. Larger = more area with amplified movement.
Control zone max scale
Movement multiplier at the bottom of the screen. Higher = small finger movements make big piece movements.
Control zone min scale
Movement multiplier at the top of the control zone. Controls the transition smoothness.
Drag snap smoothing
How smoothly pieces snap to grid cells. Lower = floaty/magnetic, Higher = instant snap.
